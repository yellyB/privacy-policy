# Privacy Policy - Slot Kitchen

**Effective date: 2026-09-08**

**Slot Kitchen** is a single-player cafe cooking/slot game. This game does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

## Data Collection

This app does **not** collect any of the following:

- Personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- Location data
- Device identifiers
- Usage analytics
- Cookies or tracking data

This app has **no internet access permission**. It runs entirely offline and never communicates with any server. No user data is sent anywhere.

Game progress (money, buff/codex unlocks, language and sound settings) is stored **locally on your device only**. It is never transmitted or shared.

This app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

This app does not collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
